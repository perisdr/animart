# Itachi × Wheatfield with Crows — Controlled One-Shot Prompt

**Visual reference:** Vincent van Gogh, *Wheatfield with Crows* (1890). Use the original painting as the compositional, color, and brushwork reference.

## Composition and landscape

Create a **16:9 landscape oil painting** that feels like Van Gogh could have painted a fleeting vision of **Itachi Uchiha from Naruto**. Preserve the original painting's broad spatial structure: immense, wind-battered yellow wheat below; a low, uneasy horizon; and an oppressive blue storm sky above. Maintain its slightly elevated viewpoint, restless perspective, raw emotional tension, and uneven, hand-painted surface. Adapt the edges naturally to widescreen without stretching, cropping away important forms, or introducing empty decorative space.

Keep wheat dominant below. Use dense strokes of golden yellow, ochre, orange, and occasional olive green to suggest stalks bending in gusts. A prominent reddish-brown earth path begins near the bottom center, curves through the wheat, and narrows toward the **central horizon**. This single emphasized path is an intentional narrative departure from Van Gogh's ambiguous diverging paths: it quietly draws the viewer toward Itachi. Let its edges remain ragged and irregular, not neatly landscaped.

## Itachi's presence

Place **Itachi Uchiha** as a **tiny, solitary, motionless figure on the distant horizon**, near the path's vanishing point. He wears a black Akatsuki cloak with a subdued red cloud motif. At that distance, communicate his identity through the dark silhouette, cloak, and red emblem—not a detailed face, giant eyes, dramatic pose, or close-up anime rendering. He should feel swallowed by the landscape: a presence discovered after the crows.

## Crows and Sharingan

Several black crows cut across the storm sky, their wings painted in heavy, angular strokes of near-black, charcoal, and dark blue. **The largest crow flies through the upper-left foreground** with outspread wings and a readable head in profile. Smaller crows arc across the middle distance toward the central sky and right side; a few are mere silhouettes around the horizon. Preserve convincing bird anatomy and atmospheric scale without detailed feathers.

The eyes imply Itachi's Sharingan, but **all markings must be made from the same crude oil-paint strokes as the birds**:

- **Closest crow:** one proportionate red eye with a recognizable, imperfect **three-tomoe Sharingan**, indicated by rough black curved dabs and irregular pigment. Clearly identifiable when examined, yet never a sharp logo or oversized disk.
- **Middle-distance crows:** small muted-crimson eyes, with at most a few broken dark strokes suggesting the Sharingan. Detail diminishes with distance.
- **Farthest crows:** tiny red flecks with a whisper of black; no readable tomoe or circular outlines.

The eyes are **pigment, not light sources**: no neon glow, glossy lenses, crisp digital circles, laser effects, or high-resolution symbols pasted over the painting.

## Sky and Akatsuki cloud

Fill the upper half with an ominous, turbulent field of cobalt, Prussian blue, indigo, and blue-black. Broad pale-blue and off-white strokes turn around a loose, disturbed spiral near the central horizon, creating a dreamlike **genjutsu distortion** without becoming a polished supernatural portal. In the **upper-right sky**, form **one large, unmistakable Akatsuki red cloud** from deep crimson, vermilion, and dark-red impasto. Its curled shape should be legible but its edges broken, uneven, and entangled with the surrounding blue brushstrokes. It belongs to the weather, not to a separate graphic layer. Avoid clean icon borders or extra floating symbols.

## Medium, mood, and priorities

Use **coarse, visible, tactile impasto** throughout: thick directional strokes, scraped pigment, blunt edges, inconsistent paint buildup, and restrained detail. Favor Van Gogh's **cruder, emotionally urgent handling** over a smooth, meticulously rendered pastiche. Let strokes visibly describe the movement of wind, wheat, birds, and clouds. No area—including the eyes, cloak emblem, or Akatsuki cloud—may look more digitally precise than the surrounding paint.

The mood is **lonely, threatening, hypnotic, and psychologically unstable**, as though the entire wheatfield is already inside Itachi's genjutsu. Keep yellow wheat, deep-blue sky, black crows, and sparse red accents in strong tension. Prioritize **composition and painterly unity first, atmospheric depth second, readable Naruto symbolism third, fine detail last**.

**Exclude:** text, signatures, frames, modern objects, extra people, oversized Itachi, excessive birds, photorealism, polished fantasy illustration, smooth airbrushing, vector-like Sharingan, geometric perfection, glowing eyes, and visual effects disconnected from the oil paint.
