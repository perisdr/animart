# Anime × Art: Complete Generation Prompt Archive

Prompts 1–12 reproduce the original generation prompts verbatim, in chronological order. Prompt 13 is a revised standalone prompt for future generation, incorporating the lessons from the sword-grip correction; it has not yet been used to generate an image.

## Generation settings and resolution

- Tool: built-in image generation (`image_gen__imagegen`).
- Every call used `transparent_background: false`.
- Prompts 1–12 were new images, without reference-image arguments.
- Prompt 13 now creates the Tanjiro × Great Wave scene from scratch without a reference image, with explicit sword-grip requirements. It replaces the earlier image-edit prompt.
- Although the prompts requested 1920 × 1080, the generated images were 1672 × 941. The downloadable 1080p versions were subsequently resized with ImageMagick using Lanczos filtering and `-resize '1920x1080!'`. That resize was a separate operation, not another generation prompt.
- Golden Boy and Mononoke concepts were suggested, but no images for them were generated in this conversation.

## 13. Tanjiro × The Great Wave — standalone prompt with correct sword grip

For a new generation without a visual reference. This rewritten prompt has not yet been tested in a generation.

```text
Create one finished widescreen 16:9 desktop wallpaper, target 1920 × 1080 pixels: Tanjiro Kamado from Demon Slayer crossed with Katsushika Hokusai’s The Great Wave off Kanagawa.

STYLE
Render the entire scene as a cohesive Japanese ukiyo-e woodblock print: indigo carved wave lines, ivory claw-shaped foam, flat pigment, fine dark contours, and subtle aged washi texture. Render Tanjiro with the same printmaking technique as the landscape.

COMPOSITION
An enormous curling wave dominates the left side, framing distant Mount Fuji beneath an open parchment-colored sky. Small traditional wooden boats ride the sea below. Tanjiro occupies a rocky outcrop in the lower-right foreground, executing a powerful two-handed Water Breathing sword slash. A sweeping ribbon of water follows the blade’s movement and merges naturally with the great wave.

TANJIRO
Preserve his recognizable burgundy hair, forehead scar, hanafuda earrings, green-and-black checkered haori, and dark Demon Slayer uniform. Give him a focused expression and a grounded, wide-legged stance, with his haori flowing behind him.

SWORD AND GRIP — ESSENTIAL
Show one continuous katana angled diagonally down-left, its handle extending up-right. Tanjiro holds it with both hands on the same wrapped handle.

Along the sword from the lower-left blade toward the upper-right pommel, the order must be:
blade → one circular guard → right hand → short visible section of wrapped handle → left hand → pommel.

His right hand grips immediately behind the guard; his left hand grips near the pommel. The guard sits entirely on the blade side of both hands, never between them. Neither hand touches the blade.

Make both hands anatomically coherent, with naturally curled fingers and opposed thumbs wrapping around the handle. Connect each wrist and forearm plausibly to its corresponding arm. Keep the blade, guard, handle, and pommel aligned as parts of one solid weapon.

Keep the grip readable: leave the guard, both hands, and the visible handle unobscured by foam, sleeves, or motion effects. The water ribbon follows the exposed blade without replacing it.

FINISH
Balance dynamic movement with the elegant clarity of a woodblock print. Use restrained indigo, ivory, parchment, and muted green, with crisp focal detail around Tanjiro and the sword.

No extra weapons, duplicate guards, disconnected blade sections, additional hands, or extra fingers. No added lettering, signatures, watermarks, or borders.
```
