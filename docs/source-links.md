## Monet exception

The local preview is the National Gallery of Art’s *The Japanese Footbridge*.
WikiArt’s `the-japanese-bridge` is a portrait-format painting;
`the-japanese-bridge-the-water-lily-pond` identifies the London painting.
The `the-japanese-bridge-the-water-lily-pond-1899` and
`the-japanese-bridge-the-bridge-in-monet-s-garden-1896` entries were visually
inspected and differ from the local preview. The first is offered only as a
clearly labelled link to another painting in the series. The primary museum URL remains.
