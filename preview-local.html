It started
with my TV.

I wanted something a little more personal on my screen. I found a new way to look at art.

After experimenting with CleanYourTV ↗ and setting up Luncher ↗, I needed wallpapers for my new launcher. Anime felt like an obvious place to start.

I don’t know much about art, and I don’t always understand it. But sometimes a painting makes me stop and look. So I started bringing familiar characters into unfamiliar paintings: something I love, mixed with something I want to understand better.

A note on AI and the originals

These wallpapers are AI-generated personal experiments. Using AI to reinterpret human-made art is controversial, and I don’t want to gloss over that. The original artworks and the people behind them are part of the story here, with credits and links to learn more.

This is an unofficial fan project. It isn’t affiliated with the artists’ estates, museums, anime creators, or rights holders. Image credits and source information are included with each piece.
